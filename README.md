## 🌟 Key Features
- **AI Combustion Intelligence:** Instantly translates complex microgravity physics (PSI-10 investigation records) into plain-English mission reports and comparative tables.
- **Strict AI Governance Guardrails:** Enforces zero-hallucination protocols, triggering automated safety refusals when queries deviate from verified NASA metadata.
- **Open Science Traceability:** Automatically embeds persistent dataset DOIs (`10.60555/w4zy-bt45`) and peer-reviewed publication links to comply with NASA Open Science standards.
- **Mission Control Theme:** Styled with official NASA deep blue palettes and immersive telemetry panels.
